#+title:      Journal Prompt
#+priority:   24

* Journal
Why: Stripped observations only. Events, deviations, reactions — no interpretation at write time. The ShapeShifter reports here; the Architect reads later.

** Default (default WARNING)
#+begin_src emacs-lisp
(defun my/manifolding-atlas-prompt-journal ()
  "Prompt for JOURNAL. Returns \"WARNING\" if skipped."
  (let ((val (completing-read
              "JOURNAL: "
              '("⚠ WARNING" "OPEN" "CLOSED" "ROTATED")
              nil t nil nil "⚠ WARNING")))
    (if (string-equal val "⚠ WARNING") "WARNING" val)))
#+end_src

** With initial value
#+begin_src emacs-lisp
(defun my/manifolding-atlas-prompt-journal-initial (initial)
  "Prompt for JOURNAL with INITIAL pre-selected."
  (completing-read
   "JOURNAL: "
   '("OPEN" "CLOSED" "ROTATED")
   nil t nil nil initial))
#+end_src

** Registration
#+begin_src emacs-lisp
(my/manifolding-atlas-register-prompt
  :key 'journal
  :name 'journal
  :label "Journal"
  :prompt-fn #'my/manifolding-atlas-prompt-journal
  :contexts '(file heading task)
  :read-current-fn (lambda () (org-entry-get nil "JOURNAL"))
  :merge-strategy 'replace
  :to-plist (lambda (val _ctx)
              (if (or (null val) (string-equal val "WARNING"))
                  `(:properties (("JOURNAL" . "WARNING")))
                `(:properties (("JOURNAL" . ,val))))))
#+end_src
