#+title:      Laws Prompt
#+priority:   27

* Laws
Why: Codified legal reality within the relevant jurisdiction. External, indifferent to intent, and non-negotiable. The Architect maps the hard walls before designing in the gray zones.

** Default (default WARNING)
#+begin_src emacs-lisp
(defun my/manifolding-atlas-prompt-laws ()
  "Prompt for LAWS. Returns \"WARNING\" if skipped."
  (let ((val (completing-read
              "LAWS: "
              '("⚠ WARNING" "RESEARCHED" "CONFIRMED" "CHANGED" "REVISITED")
              nil t nil nil "⚠ WARNING")))
    (if (string-equal val "⚠ WARNING") "WARNING" val)))
#+end_src

** With initial value
#+begin_src emacs-lisp
(defun my/manifolding-atlas-prompt-laws-initial (initial)

   nil t nil nil initial))

** Registration
#+begin_src emacs-lisp
(my/manifolding-atlas-register-prompt
  :key 'laws
  :name 'laws
  :label "Laws"
  :prompt-fn #'my/manifolding-atlas-prompt-laws
  :contexts '(file heading task)
  :read-current-fn (lambda () (org-entry-get nil "LAWS"))
  :merge-strategy 'replace
  :to-plist (lambda (val _ctx)
              (if (or (null val) (string-equal val "WARNING"))
                  `(:properties (("LAWS" . "WARNING")))
                `(:properties (("LAWS" . ,val))))))
#+end_src
