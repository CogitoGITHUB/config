#+title:      Signals Prompt
#+priority:   33

* Signals
Why: Live leading indicators being actively watched. Not evidence — retrospective. Not hypotheses — open questions. Inputs arriving now that may become either.

** Default (default WARNING)
#+begin_src emacs-lisp
(defun my/manifolding-atlas-prompt-signals ()
  "Prompt for SIGNALS. Returns \"WARNING\" if skipped."
  (let ((val (completing-read
              "SIGNALS: "
              '("⚠ WARNING" "WATCHED" "RESOLVED" "IGNORED")
              nil t nil nil "⚠ WARNING")))
    (if (string-equal val "⚠ WARNING") "WARNING" val)))
#+end_src

** With initial value
#+begin_src emacs-lisp
(defun my/manifolding-atlas-prompt-signals-initial (initial)
  "Prompt for SIGNALS with INITIAL pre-selected."
  (completing-read
   "SIGNALS: "
   '("WATCHED" "RESOLVED" "IGNORED")
   nil t nil nil initial))
#+end_src

** Registration
#+begin_src emacs-lisp
(my/manifolding-atlas-register-prompt
  :key 'signals
  :name 'signals
  :label "Signals"
  :prompt-fn #'my/manifolding-atlas-prompt-signals
  :contexts '(file heading task)
  :read-current-fn (lambda () (org-entry-get nil "SIGNALS"))
  :merge-strategy 'replace
  :to-plist (lambda (val _ctx)
              (if (or (null val) (string-equal val "WARNING"))
                  `(:properties (("SIGNALS" . "WARNING")))
                `(:properties (("SIGNALS" . ,val))))))
#+end_src
