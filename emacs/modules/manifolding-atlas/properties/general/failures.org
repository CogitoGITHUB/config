#+title:      Failures Prompt
#+priority:   37

* Failures
Why: Retrospective post-mortems only. Not predictions — records. What failed, the exact mechanism of failure, what it cost, and what the Architect updates in response.

** Default (default WARNING)
#+begin_src emacs-lisp
(defun my/manifolding-atlas-prompt-failures ()
  "Prompt for FAILURES. Returns \"WARNING\" if skipped."
  (let ((val (completing-read
              "FAILURES: "
              '("⚠ WARNING" "RECORDED" "ANALYZED" "ACTIVE")
              nil t nil nil "⚠ WARNING")))
    (if (string-equal val "⚠ WARNING") "WARNING" val)))
#+end_src

** With initial value
#+begin_src emacs-lisp
(defun my/manifolding-atlas-prompt-failures-initial (initial)
  "Prompt for FAILURES with INITIAL pre-selected."
  (completing-read
   "FAILURES: "
   '("RECORDED" "ANALYZED" "ACTIVE")
   nil t nil nil initial))
#+end_src

** Registration
#+begin_src emacs-lisp
(my/manifolding-atlas-register-prompt
  :key 'failures
  :name 'failures
  :label "Failures"
  :prompt-fn #'my/manifolding-atlas-prompt-failures
  :contexts '(file heading task)
  :read-current-fn (lambda () (org-entry-get nil "FAILURES"))
  :merge-strategy 'replace
  :to-plist (lambda (val _ctx)
              (if (or (null val) (string-equal val "WARNING"))
                  `(:properties (("FAILURES" . "WARNING")))
                `(:properties (("FAILURES" . ,val))))))
#+end_src
