#+title:      Traps External Prompt
#+priority:   36

* Traps External
Why: Traps authored for opposing actors. Positions, framings, and conditions that limit their options or force unfavorable moves. Constructed deliberately — never reactively.

** Default (default WARNING)
#+begin_src emacs-lisp
(defun my/manifolding-atlas-prompt-traps-external ()
  "Prompt for TRAPS_EXTERNAL. Returns \"WARNING\" if skipped."
  (let ((val (completing-read
              "TRAPS_EXTERNAL: "
              '("⚠ WARNING" "DESIGNED" "SPRUNG" "RETIRED")
              nil t nil nil "⚠ WARNING")))
    (if (string-equal val "⚠ WARNING") "WARNING" val)))
#+end_src

** With initial value
#+begin_src emacs-lisp
(defun my/manifolding-atlas-prompt-traps-external-initial (initial)
  "Prompt for TRAPS_EXTERNAL with INITIAL pre-selected."
  (completing-read
   "TRAPS_EXTERNAL: "
   '("DESIGNED" "SPRUNG" "RETIRED")
   nil t nil nil initial))
#+end_src

** Registration
#+begin_src emacs-lisp
(my/manifolding-atlas-register-prompt
  :key 'traps-external
  :name 'traps-external
  :label "Traps External"
  :prompt-fn #'my/manifolding-atlas-prompt-traps-external
  :contexts '(file heading task)
  :read-current-fn (lambda () (org-entry-get nil "TRAPS_EXTERNAL"))
  :merge-strategy 'replace
  :to-plist (lambda (val _ctx)
              (if (or (null val) (string-equal val "WARNING"))
                  `(:properties (("TRAPS_EXTERNAL" . "WARNING")))
                `(:properties (("TRAPS_EXTERNAL" . ,val))))))
#+end_src
