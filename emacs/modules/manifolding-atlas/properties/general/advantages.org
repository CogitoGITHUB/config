* Advantages
Why: Structural edges, asymmetries, and positional strengths that increase the probability of success. Honest inventory only — what is real versus what is flattering.

** Default (default WARNING)
#+begin_src emacs-lisp
(defun my/manifolding-atlas-prompt-advantages ()
  "Prompt for ADVANTAGES. Returns \"WARNING\" if skipped."
  (let ((val (completing-read
              "ADVANTAGES: "
              '("⚠ WARNING" "VERIFIED" "ASSUMED" "EXPIRED")
              nil t nil nil "⚠ WARNING")))
    (if (string-equal val "⚠ WARNING") "WARNING" val)))
#+end_src

** With initial value
#+begin_src emacs-lisp
(defun my/manifolding-atlas-prompt-advantages-initial (initial)
  "Prompt for ADVANTAGES with INITIAL pre-selected."

   nil t nil nil initial))

** Registration
#+begin_src emacs-lisp
(my/manifolding-atlas-register-prompt
  :key 'advantages
  :name 'advantages
  :label "Advantages"
  :prompt-fn #'my/manifolding-atlas-prompt-advantages
  :contexts '(file heading task)
  :read-current-fn (lambda () (org-entry-get nil "ADVANTAGES"))
  :merge-strategy 'replace
  :to-plist (lambda (val _ctx)
              (if (or (null val) (string-equal val "WARNING"))
                  `(:properties (("ADVANTAGES" . "WARNING")))
                `(:properties (("ADVANTAGES" . ,val))))))
#+end_src
