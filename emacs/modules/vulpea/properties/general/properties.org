#+title:      Extra Properties Prompt
#+priority:   1

* Extra Properties
Why: Store arbitrary key-value metadata on notes. Used by vulpea queries,
org queries, and custom workflows. Press blank key to stop adding more.
Properties become queryable in the vulpea DB.

** Default (loop until blank key)
#+begin_src emacs-lisp
(defun my/vulpea-prompt-properties ()
  "Prompt for extra property key-value pairs.
Returns an alist of (key . value).  Press blank key to finish."
  (let (props)
    (catch 'done
      (while t
        (let ((k (read-string "Property key (blank to stop): ")))
          (if (string-empty-p k)
              (throw 'done props)
            (push (cons k (read-string (format "Value for %s: " k)))
                  props)))))
    (nreverse props)))
#+end_src

** Registration
#+begin_src emacs-lisp
(my/vulpea-register-prompt
  :key 'properties
  :name 'properties
  :label "Extra Properties"
  :prompt-fn #'my/vulpea-prompt-properties
  :contexts '(file heading)
  :read-current-fn nil
  :merge-strategy 'merge
  :to-plist (lambda (val _ctx)
              (when val `(:properties ,val))))
#+end_src
