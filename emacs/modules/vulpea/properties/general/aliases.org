#+title:      Aliases Prompt
#+priority:   1

* Aliases
Why: Multiple ways to find the same note. If you refer to something by different
names in different contexts, aliases let any of them work in ~vulpea-find~.
Comma-separated, blank = no aliases.

** Default (comma-separated string)
#+begin_src emacs-lisp
(defun my/vulpea-prompt-aliases ()
  "Prompt for comma-separated aliases.  Returns nil or a list of strings."
  (let* ((raw (read-string "Aliases (comma-separated, or blank): "))
         (parts (split-string raw "," t "[ \t]+")))
    (unless (or (string-empty-p raw) (null parts))
      parts)))
#+end_src

** Registration
#+begin_src emacs-lisp
(my/vulpea-register-prompt
  :key 'aliases
  :name 'aliases
  :label "Aliases"
  :prompt-fn #'my/vulpea-prompt-aliases
  :contexts '(file heading)
  :read-current-fn (lambda () (org-entry-get nil "ALIAS"))
  :merge-strategy 'replace
  :to-plist (lambda (val _ctx)
              (when val
                (let ((labels (copy-sequence val)))
                  `(:properties (("ALIAS" . ,(mapconcat #'identity val ", ")))
                    :post-apply (,(apply-partially
                                   #'my/vulpea--apply-aliases-post labels)))))))
#+end_src
